## Answer

**1. Status: resolved (yes).** This is the Hajnal–Szemerédi theorem.

- **Who and when:** András Hajnal and Endre Szemerédi, "Proof of a conjecture of P. Erdős," in *Combinatorial Theory and its Applications* (1970).
- **Earlier case:** r = 3 was proved by Corrádi and Hajnal in 1963.
- **Answer:** Yes. Every graph on rm vertices with minimum degree at least m(r−1) contains m vertex-disjoint copies of K_r.
- **Key idea:** Pass to the complement.
  - The complement has maximum degree at most rm − 1 − m(r−1) = m − 1.
  - The statement then becomes equitable colouring: a graph with maximum degree Δ has an equitable (Δ+1)-colouring.
  - Here that means m colour classes, each of size exactly r.
  - Each class is independent in the complement, so it is a K_r in the original graph.
- **Proof technique:** The original proof is an intricate recoloring and induction argument. Kierstead and Kostochka (2008) gave a much shorter proof, also using a recoloring and discharging-style argument.

**2. Guess:** Not needed, since I recall the resolution.

**3. Sources:**

- Kierstead–Kostochka, "A short proof of the Hajnal–Szemerédi theorem on equitable colouring," *Combinatorics, Probability and Computing* (2008).
