## Answer

1. **Status.** I believe this has been resolved, with the answer **yes**. I attribute it to Vojtěch Rödl, in a paper from around 1977, "On the chromatic number of subgraphs of a given graph" (Proc. AMS). I think the argument is fairly elementary and combinatorial, but I do not reliably remember its details. The most I recall is that it plays off neighbourhoods of large chromatic number against extracting a bipartite-like triangle-free structure, possibly by iterating. I would not claim that description is accurate.

   The broader Erdős–Hajnal question is, as far as I know, still open. It asks whether large chromatic number forces a subgraph with both large chromatic number and large girth, or even just no $C_4$. Only the triangle-free case asked here was settled.

2. **Expected answer.** Not applicable, since I recall a resolution. If I am misremembering, I would still guess yes.

3. **Source.** V. Rödl, "On the chromatic number of subgraphs of a given graph," Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. (c. 1977). I also recall that erdosproblems.com marks this problem as proved. I cannot verify either point here.
