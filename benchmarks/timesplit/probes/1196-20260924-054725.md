   - This is the "asymptotic" version of the Erdős primitive set conjecture.
   - Lichtman proved the non-asymptotic version in 2022: the sum over a primitive set is at most the sum over the primes.
   - My memory is that the asymptotic bound 1+o(1) for A ⊂ [x,∞) was proved in 2026, reportedly with substantial AI assistance (a GPT-model-generated argument). I recall Lichtman and others checking or commenting on it.

It assigns each integer a "stopping" measure, so that the weights 1/(a log a) of a primitive set are dominated by the probabilities of disjoint events, which total 1+o(1). This refines Erdős's original disjoint-multiples argument and Lichtman's method.
