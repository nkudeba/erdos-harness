I have a vague memory that the erdosproblems.com page now lists #204 as resolved in the negative, meaning no such $n$ exists, possibly in a 2025 arXiv preprint by an author named Adenwalla. 2. **Expected answer (a guess).** No. Requiring every pair of classes whose moduli share a factor to be disjoint is very restrictive. A density count gives only $\sum_{d\mid n,\,d>1} 1/d = \sigma(n)/n - 1$. That sum can exceed 1 when $n$ is abundant, so the count alone does not rule anything out. But the classes for $d$ and $d'$ must still be disjoint whenever $\gcd(d,d')>1$, and the divisors of $n$ are heavily nested. That should prevent a full cover.
