Here is why:
- Write ν_p for the exponent of the prime p. Then τ((n+1)!)/τ(n!) = ∏_{p | n+1} (1 + ν_p(n+1)/(ν_p(n!)+1)).
- Since ν_p(n!) is about n/p, a prime factor p contributes a non-negligible factor only if p is comparable to n.

- The case k = 1 (n+1 prime) gives exactly 2.
- Letting k → ∞, or taking n+1 with no large prime factor, gives 1.
