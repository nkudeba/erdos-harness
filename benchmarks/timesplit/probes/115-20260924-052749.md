## Answer

1. **Status: I believe it is resolved, in the affirmative (yes).**
   - Pommerenke first proved an upper bound of $(e/2)n^2$ for polynomials whose lemniscate $\{|p|\le 1\}$ is connected.
   - Eremenko and Lempert then proved the sharp bound $\tfrac12 n^2$ in "An extremal problem for polynomials" (Proc. AMS, 1994).
   - The Chebyshev polynomials $T_n$ attain the bound. Here $\{|T_n|\le 1\}$ contains $[-1,1]$, and $|T_n'(\pm1)| = n^2$, which is $\tfrac12 n^2$ after the normalization used in the problem. So the Chebyshev polynomials are extremal and the $o(1)$ term is unnecessary.
   - Key idea, as I recall it: a potential-theoretic and extremal argument. It works with the Green's function and conformal structure of the complement of the connected filled lemniscate. It shows the extremal configuration is the Chebyshev one, where the critical values are real and $\pm1$.

3. The source I recall is A. Eremenko and L. Lempert, "An extremal problem for polynomials," Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 122 (1994). I believe erdosproblems.com lists #115 as solved with this reference.
