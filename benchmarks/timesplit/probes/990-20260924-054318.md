1. **Status.** I do not know of a resolution. As far as I know, the problem is still open.

   The known baseline is the Erdős–Turán theorem. It gives this discrepancy bound with d, the degree, in place of n: |(# θᵢ ∈ I) − |I|d/2π| ≪ (d log M)^{1/2}. The question is whether d can be replaced by n, the number of non-zero coefficients.

These would bound the number of zeros in small sectors, or near the positive real axis, in terms of the number of terms. I cannot reliably say whether he proved the full statement, or whether a counterexample is known.
