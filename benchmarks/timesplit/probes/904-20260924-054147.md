## Answer

1. **Status.** I believe this has been resolved affirmatively (the answer is "yes"), but I'm only moderately confident.
   - **Who and when.** My recollection is that Bollobás and Nikiforov proved it in the mid-2000s. I cannot vouch for the exact paper.
   - **Earlier case.** I think the triangle case r = 2 was handled earlier, by Edwards and independently by Khadžiev–Nikiforov. The r = 2 statement is that m ≥ n²/4 forces an edge xy with d(x)+d(y) ≥ 4m/n.
   - **Key idea.** I don't reliably remember the method. My vague sense is that it counts cliques weighted by degrees, possibly with a spectral or Motzkin–Straus-type averaging argument, but I can't reconstruct it.

2. **Expected answer.** If I'm wrong about the resolution, I would still guess "yes". The complete (r-partite) Turán graph T_r(n) is the natural extremal example: every vertex there has degree about 2m/n. Adding edges should only help.

3. **Source.** I have a hazy recollection of a Bollobás–Nikiforov paper, possibly "Cliques and the spectral radius" (JCTB, 2007) or a related paper on joints and cliques. I can't be sure which paper, or that the attribution is right.
