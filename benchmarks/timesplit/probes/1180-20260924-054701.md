The question comes from Erdős and Graham and concerns sums of modular inverses of small integers. The closest work I can recall is:

- **Shparlinski**, possibly a paper titled something like "On a question of Erdős and Graham," using exponential sums over modular inverses (Kloosterman-type sums). I vaguely associate it with results showing every residue is a sum of a bounded number of inverses 1/n, with n up to some power of p.

Sum-product and additive-combinatorics methods (Bourgain-style expansion) usually show that such "multiplicatively structured" sets grow quickly under repeated addition. After a bounded number of steps, depending on ε, the sumset should cover all of Z/pZ.
