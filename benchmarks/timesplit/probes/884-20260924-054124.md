2. **Guess: no.** This is a guess, not a remembered result.
   - Take n = lcm(1,…,m). Its divisors include every integer from 1 to m.
   - Among these small divisors, the consecutive gaps are all 1. They contribute about m to the right-hand side.
   - The pairs among them contribute about Σ_{i<j≤m} 1/(j−i) ≈ m log m to the left-hand side.
   - More generally, in any region where divisors are roughly evenly spaced, the full pair sum seems to beat the consecutive-gap sum by about a log factor.
   - The catch is that the right-hand side counts the whole range of divisors. Other regions could contain extremely close consecutive divisors that make it large, and I have not checked this.
