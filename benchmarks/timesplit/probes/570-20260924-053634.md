## Answer

1. **Status.** As far as I know, this problem is still **open** in general.

   It is a conjecture of Erdős, Faudree, Rousseau and Schelp. The only case I recall with any confidence is $k=3$. For $k=3$ the bound reads $R(K_3,H)\le 2m+1$, and this was proved by Sidorenko, with an independent proof by Goddard and Kleitman. I have a vague and unreliable memory of partial results for some other small $k$, or under extra structural assumptions on $H$. I know of no resolution for all $k\ge 3$.

   - The $k=3$ case holds.
   - The bound matches natural constructions, so it is plausibly tight.
   - Fixed-cycle-versus-sparse-graph Ramsey problems tend to have "linear in size" behaviour of this kind.

   This is a guess, not a recalled result.
