It says that if f also satisfies a regularity condition such as measurability, then f must be monotone, in fact non-decreasing. As I remember it, the open question is whether that extra assumption can be dropped. I am not certain whether erdosproblems.com currently lists #1125 as open or solved, so my status is "unsure".

   - An additive function that is non-negative on (0, ∞) is automatically linear, so it is monotone.
   - So the standard trick produces no counterexample. This suggests the condition may be strong enough to force monotonicity without any measurability assumption.
