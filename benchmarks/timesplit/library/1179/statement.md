Let $0<\epsilon<1$ and let $g_\epsilon(N)$ be the minimal $k$ such that if $G$ is an abelian group of size $N$ and $A\subseteq G$ is a uniformly random subset of size $k$, and\[F_A(g) = \#\left\{ S\subseteq A : g = \sum_{x\in S}x\right\},\]then, with probability $\to 1$ as $N\to \infty$,\[\left\lvert F_A(g)-\frac{2^k}{N}\right\rvert \leq \epsilon \frac{2^k}{N}\]for all $g\in G$. Estimate $g_\epsilon(N)$ - in particular, is it true that for all $\epsilon>0$\[g_\epsilon(N)=(1+o_\epsilon(1))\log_2N?\]
