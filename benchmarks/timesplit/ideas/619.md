# 619

Is there a constant c > 0 such that every connected triangle-free graph on n vertices can be brought to diameter at most 4, while staying triangle-free, by adding fewer than (1 − c)n edges?

**Answer:** false

**Sources:** erdosproblems.com/619 (cached page); forum thread /forum/thread/619: Kuhn's summary of the Claude Fable 5 construction with the Lean formalisation repository (14 Jun 2026), Bloom's check and parameter optimisation (15 Jun 2026), and Tang's note that "diameter r" means diameter at most r (29 Apr 2026); proof-claims page (empty); pool.json (the formal statement uses ediam ≤ 4 and CliqueFree 3).

**Key idea:** Take a triangle-free core H on m vertices with maximum degree d and independence number α(H) = O((m/d)·log d), which a standard random first-moment construction provides, and hang s ≈ 4/η pendant leaves on each core vertex. In any triangle-free supergraph of diameter at most 4, two pendant components that have no new edge to the core must reach each other through the core, so their core anchors are within distance 2 of each other. Triangle-freeness keeps every neighbourhood an independent set, so each added edge creates only O(α(H)) new close core pairs. Close pairs therefore stay scarce, and almost all of the n − m pendants need an added edge of their own.

**Main steps:**
- Build H (triangle-free, maximum degree d, α(H) ≪ (m/d)·log d) and let G be H with s pendants attached to each core vertex, so n = (s+1)·m. Choose d, and then m, large in terms of η.
- Let G' be a triangle-free supergraph of diameter at most 4 obtained by adding t edges, and let C_P be the set of components of the pendant-induced subgraph of G' with no new edge to the core. A spanning-forest count gives t ≥ n − m − |C_P|.
- Any two components in C_P are joined by a path of length at most 4 through the core, so their anchors are within distance 2. Hence |C_P|² ≤ s²·#(core pairs at distance ≤ 2 in G').
- There are O(m·d²) such pairs at the start, and each new edge adds O(α(H)), because neighbourhoods are independent. After at most n new edges there are O(m·d² + n·α(H)) pairs, so |C_P| ≤ ηn/4 for the chosen parameters.
- So t > (1 − η)·n. Optimising the parameters gives h₄(G) ≥ n − O(n^{8/9}·(log n)^{2/9}) for infinitely many n.

**A hit looks like:** the notes build a pendant (leaf) blow-up of a triangle-free, bounded-degree core with small independence number. They argue that triangle-freeness limits how many new distance-2 pairs each added edge can create, so most leaves need their own new edge.

**A partial hit looks like:** leaf blow-ups of simple cores (paths, cycles, stars, bipartite graphs) without control of the independence number; a restatement of the Erdős–Gyárfás–Ruszinkó bounds h₃ ≤ n and h₅ ≤ (n−1)/2; or recognising that the triangle-free requirement is what blocks hub-style shortcuts, without a quantitative core construction.

**Confidence:** high. The prompter summarises the construction on the forum, Bloom checked it, and it is formalised in Lean.
