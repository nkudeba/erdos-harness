# 997

For every real α, is the sequence of fractional parts {α·p_n} (p_n the n-th prime) not well-distributed, meaning that windows of k consecutive terms, taken anywhere in the sequence, do not become uniformly equidistributed as k grows?

**Answer:** true

**Sources:** erdosproblems.com/997 (cached page); forum thread /forum/thread/997: Tao's comment of 1 Apr 2026, comparing the proof with Benatar's 2013 Bohr-set approach, and Monticone's Aristotle formalisation, which takes the Maynard–Tao/Banks–Freiberg–Turnage-Butterbaugh theorem as an axiom; proof-claims page (empty); Alexeev–Putterman–Sawhney–Sellke–Valiant, arXiv:2603.29961, §4 ("Fractional parts of {αp}"), including its statement of Banks–Freiberg–Turnage-Butterbaugh (Acta Arith. 2015) as Theorem 4.2.

**Key idea:** The proof uses clusters of consecutive primes in one residue class. Banks–Freiberg–Turnage-Butterbaugh, a consequence of Maynard–Tao, gives for every m and every (a, q) = 1 infinitely many runs of m consecutive primes, all ≡ a (mod q), spanning at most q·C_m, with C_m independent of q. Take a Dirichlet approximation |α − a/q| ≤ 1/(qQ) with Q = ⌈C_m/δ⌉. Differences within such a run are multiples of q of size at most q·C_m, so all the values α·p_{r+j} agree mod 1 to within δ, and m consecutive terms sit in an interval of width δ, which well-distribution forbids.

**Main steps:**
- Fix δ < 1/2 and m. Dirichlet's theorem gives q ≤ Q = ⌈C_m/δ⌉ and a with (a, q) = 1 and |α − a/q| ≤ 1/(qQ).
- By BFT15 there are infinitely many r with p_{r+1} ≡ ⋯ ≡ p_{r+m} ≡ a (mod q) and p_{r+m} − p_{r+1} ≤ q·C_m.
- For 1 ≤ j, j' ≤ m, ‖α·(p_{r+j} − p_{r+j'})‖ ≤ q·C_m/(qQ) ≤ δ, so the m values {α·p_{r+j}} lie in an arc of length δ.
- A window of k = m consecutive terms then misses an interval of length 1 − δ entirely, so its discrepancy is at least (1 − δ)·k, for arbitrarily large k. This works for every real α, rational or irrational.

**A hit looks like:** the notes use bounded-gap technology for consecutive primes (Maynard–Tao), specifically runs of consecutive primes in a common residue class with span O_m(q) uniformly in q (BFT15), together with a rational approximation a/q of α, so that m consecutive values of α·p_n cluster mod 1.

**A partial hit looks like:** only the existence of one bad α (Champagne–Lê–Liu–Wooley 2024); only rational α; Benatar-style Maynard-sieve clusters in Bohr sets, which give only Diophantine α; Maynard–Tao clusters of primes that are not consecutive, although the definition needs consecutive indices; or Shiu-type strings of consecutive primes in a residue class without control of their span in terms of q.

**Confidence:** high. The proof is a few lines in §4 of the paper once BFT15 is granted, Tao's forum comment matches it, and it is formalised in Lean with BFT15 as an axiom.
