## Answer

1. **Recognition.** Yes. This is the **Erdős–Heilbronn conjecture**, restricted-sumset form. It says that for a prime ℓ and A ⊆ ℤ/ℓℤ, the restricted sumset |A ∔ A| = |{a + b : a, b ∈ A, a ≠ b}| is at least min(ℓ, 2|A| − 3).
   - Erdős and Heilbronn posed it in the 1960s, and it appears in Erdős–Graham's problem collections.
   - It was first proved by **J. A. Dias da Silva and Y. O. Hamidoune** (Bull. London Math. Soc., 1994), using exterior algebra and representation theory.
   - A much simpler proof by the polynomial method was given by **N. Alon, M. B. Nathanson and I. Ruzsa** (Amer. Math. Monthly 1995, and J. Number Theory 1996).
   - I am highly confident (about 95%) in this identification. The Lean statement matches it, and the trivial cases |B| ≤ 1 make the left side negative, so they hold automatically.

2. **Strategy.** I would use the Alon–Nathanson–Ruzsa argument via Alon's Combinatorial Nullstellensatz. I remember this strategy from the source; I am not inventing it.
   - Let k = |B|.
   - **Reduction when 2k − 3 > ℓ.** Pass to a subset B′ ⊆ B with 2|B′| − 3 = ℓ. Such a subset exists because ℓ is odd; the case ℓ = 2 is checked by hand.
   - **Main case, 2k − 3 ≤ ℓ.** Suppose for contradiction that the restricted sumset lies in a set C with |C| = 2k − 4, padding C if necessary.
   - Consider f(x, y) = (x − y)·∏_{c∈C}(x + y − c). It has degree 2k − 3 and vanishes on all of B × B.
   - The coefficient of x^{k−1}y^{k−2} in f is C(2k−4, k−2) − C(2k−4, k−1). This is, up to sign, a ballot/Catalan number. Its prime factors are all at most 2k − 4 < ℓ, so it is nonzero in 𝔽_ℓ.
   - The Nullstellensatz then says f cannot vanish on B × B, which is the contradiction.

3. **Existing proofs.**
   - The published proofs are the two above. The ANR proof also appears in Alon's "Combinatorial Nullstellensatz" (Combin. Probab. Comput., 1999) and in Tao–Vu's *Additive Combinatorics*.
   - I do not remember a specific Lean or Mathlib formalization of Erdős–Heilbronn.
   - Mathlib does contain Cauchy–Davenport (e.g. `ZMod.min_le_card_add`). I believe a Combinatorial Nullstellensatz for `MvPolynomial` was added more recently, but I am not certain of its exact name or form.
