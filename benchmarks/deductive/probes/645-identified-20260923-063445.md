I associate the proof with Brown and Landman, "Monochromatic arithmetic progressions with large differences," Bull. Austral. Math. Soc., around 1999. I am fairly confident (about 75%) that the answer is "yes" and that the problem is catalogued as solved. I am less confident (about 55%) about the exact attribution and year.

The Lean statement matches the informal one, with a positivity condition 0 < x. That condition is harmless, and it is needed: otherwise x = 0 with c 0 = c d = c (2d) becomes easier or trivial for some colourings.

The progressions with x = 1 (1, 1+d, 1+2d for d ≥ 2) and x = 2 (2, 2+d, 2+2d for d ≥ 3) quickly force colours along chains.
