  A partial proof claimed by Lloyd.H (using Gpt 5.6 Sol Ultra), 2026-07-17: We claim that there is a countably infinite set \(A\subseteq\mathbb R^2\) in full general position whose integer-distance graph is a disjoint union of finite connected triangle-free graphs with unbounded chromatic numbers.
