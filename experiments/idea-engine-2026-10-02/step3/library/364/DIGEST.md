 Regarding the cube case and the general problem: the two remaining mixed shapes, x^3-1=p^2a^3 with x^3+1=q^3z^2, and x^3-1=p^3y^2 with x^3+1=q^2b^3 (p, q prime), are excluded in this preprint (reduction to t^6+t^3+1=3w^2, solved through an elliptic quotient of rank 0).
A further collected volume with three parts is available on Zenodo (concept DOIs, all versions):
- Volume: 10.5281/zenodo.23127546
- Part I, triples (Pell addresses, a height inequality, witnesses): 10.5281/zenodo.23127702
- Part II, pairs and near-consecutive pairs: 10.5281/zenodo.23127704
- Part III, gaps, cyclotomic values, independence tests: 10.5281/zenodo.23127706
Data and scripts: GitHub repository. These are partial results and not a solution of #364. AI assistance is disclosed in the preprints.
