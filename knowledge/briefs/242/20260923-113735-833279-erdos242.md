## What the problem asks

Take any whole number n larger than 2. The question is whether 4/n can always be written as a sum of three fractions of the form 1/x, where x is a whole number and the three x's are all different. Such a fraction, with 1 on top, is called a unit fraction. For example, 4/3 = 1/1 + 1/4 + 1/12. The catalogue lists the problem as open and "falsifiable". That means a single number n with no such sum would disprove it. The claim must hold for every n, and nobody has shown that it does.
