## What changed on 2026-09-16 (experiments/699-units-digit/REPORT.md)

A counterexample at i=3 implies the units-digit system
    (U3)  A' | j,  B' | j(j-1),  C' | j(j-1)(j-2),
where A',B',C' are the odd parts of n, n-1, n-2 with one factor 3 removed when 3 exactly divides (3^e, e>=2, is
kept). Higher Lucas digits are NOT used. (U3) has no solution for any n with v_2(n) <= 38 (hence none below 2^39 ≈ 5.5e11)
and its written reduction gives, for any solution: 4 | n, j < n/2, and A'^2 < 4.5h + 2.25 with h = n/(2A'),
i.e. the power of 2 dividing n is at least about n^(2/3)/4. This replaces the 2026-09-13 bound n <= (9/4)4^r + 1.
The same phenomenon holds for i = 4..10 (system U(i), no solutions to n = 10^6). All of this is WRITTEN and
exactly checked, not Lean-certified.
